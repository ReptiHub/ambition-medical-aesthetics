Botox vs. Dysport: Which Is Right for You?

The Short AnswerBoth Botox and Dysport are FDA-approved neuromodulators that smooth expression lines by relaxing targeted muscles. Dysport tends to kick in slightly faster (2–3 days vs 4–7) and diffuses more, which can suit larger areas like the forehead. Botox is more concentrated and precise, ideal for smaller zones. Results from both last roughly 3–4 months. The right choice depends on your anatomy and goals — which is exactly what a consultation at Ambition Medical Aesthetics in Campbell, CA determines.

First, what do they actually do?

Botox and Dysport are both neuromodulators — purified proteins that temporarily relax the tiny muscles responsible for expression lines. When those muscles rest, the skin above them smooths. Frown lines soften, crow's feet fade, and the forehead looks rested instead of furrowed.

Neither is a filler. They don't add volume; they simply quiet the repetitive movements that etch lines into the skin over time. Used well, they're also preventative — softening lines before they become permanent creases.

The key differences

Onset: how fast you'll see results

Dysport is typically the sprinter, with visible softening in about 2–3 days. Botox usually takes 4–7 days to fully settle. If you have an event on the calendar, that difference can matter.

Spread: precision vs. coverage

Dysport diffuses a little more from each injection point, which makes it efficient for broader areas like the full forehead. Botox stays more concentrated where it's placed — a scalpel-fine tool for smaller, precise zones like crow's feet or a lip flip.

Longevity

Both last roughly 3–4 months for most people, influenced by your metabolism, dose, and the area treated. With regular maintenance, many clients find results begin to last longer.

So which one should you choose?

  • Choose Dysport if: you want fast onset, you're treating a broad area like the forehead, or you've felt Botox results were too "stiff" in the past.
  • Choose Botox if: you want pinpoint precision in smaller areas, you've had consistent results with it before, or your injector recommends it for your muscle pattern.

Honestly? The product matters less than the hands holding the syringe. Dosing, placement, and an eye for natural movement are what separate a refreshed face from a frozen one. At Ambition Medical Aesthetics, we favor conservative, precise dosing — you should look like you, on your best day.

What to expect at your appointment

A consultation and treatment typically take 20–30 minutes. You'll feel a few quick pinches — most clients rate it far below an eyebrow wax. There's no downtime; just skip strenuous exercise and avoid rubbing the area for 24 hours. Results build over the following days.
